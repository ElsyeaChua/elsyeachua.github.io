Featured project

Saltique: modern salt bread

Visit @saltique.id on Instagram →
What I took away: how a business idea turns into a real product, and how branding and talking directly with customers shape sales.

Saltique is a group entrepreneurship project developed as part of my university's Entrepreneurship specialization. My team and I applied what we learned in class by developing and managing a real business.

We focused on modern salt bread, aiming to introduce an appealing bakery product through a variety of flavours and a distinctive brand concept.

  • Business concept

    Shaped the idea, the product focus on modern salt bread and its range of flavours.

  • Brand identity

    Built a distinctive brand concept so the product stands out to customers.

  • Product preparation

    Prepared the products and kept quality consistent for sale.

  • Direct sales

    Sold directly to customers, explaining the product and handling transactions.
